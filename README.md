Modern e-commerce and logistics operations involve multiple warehouses, inventory locations, shipping carriers, and delivery stages. Managing these processes manually or through disconnected systems can lead to inefficient inventory allocation, unnecessary shipping costs, warehouse workload imbalance, delayed shipments, and slow responses to operational exceptions.

The proposed system aims to develop a distributed logistics orchestration platform that automatically coordinates the order-fulfillment lifecycle—from order placement and inventory allocation to warehouse processing, carrier selection, shipment tracking, delivery exceptions, and returns.

The core problem isn't "shipping a product."

It's coordinating thousands of dependent logistics operations reliably and automatically across multiple services and physical locations.
