1. 📦 Inventory Service — “Do we have the stock?”

Its only concern is inventory.

Responsibilities:

Maintain how much stock exists at each warehouse.
Check whether an item is available.
Reserve stock for an order.
Release reserved stock if an order is cancelled.
Increase/decrease stock when inventory changes.
Tell other services whether reservation succeeded or failed.

Example:

Order needs 2 iPhones.

Inventory Service checks:
Warehouse A → 5 available
Warehouse B → 0 available
It reserves 2 from Warehouse A.

Output/event:

INVENTORY_RESERVED
warehouse = A
quantity = 2

It does NOT decide:

how the item should be picked
which courier should deliver it
how the shipment is tracked

===========================================================================

2. 🏭 Warehouse Service — “How do we physically process the package?”

Its concern is warehouse operations.

Responsibilities:

Know which warehouse needs to process an order.
Create picking tasks.
Assign/track warehouse tasks.
Track picking.
Track packing.
Mark package as ready for dispatch.
Maintain warehouse/task status.

Example:

Inventory says:
"2 iPhones reserved at Warehouse A"

        ↓

Warehouse Service

        ↓

Create:
PICK_TASK #102
→ Pick 2 iPhones from Warehouse A

        ↓

Items picked

        ↓

Package packed

        ↓

PACKAGE_READY

It does NOT:

manage stock quantities
choose the courier
create the final shipment

==============================================================

3. 🚚 Carrier Selection Engine — “Which courier should we use?”

This one is different.

It is primarily a decision-making service.

It receives information such as:

Origin: Pune
Destination: Mumbai
Weight: 2 kg
Delivery requirement: 2 days

Then considers available carriers:

Delhivery
Blue Dart
DTDC
...

and applies our business rules.

For example:

IF delivery_time <= 2 days
AND cost < ₹200
AND carrier serves destination
→ eligible

Then it produces:

CARRIER_SELECTED

carrier = Delhivery

Its responsibility is selection, not shipment management.

It does NOT:

maintain inventory
pick packages
track shipments
create warehouse tasks

==========================================================================

4. 🚛 Shipment Service — “Manage the shipment after a carrier is chosen”

Once the carrier is selected, Shipment Service takes over.

Responsibilities:

Create a shipment.
Generate/maintain shipment ID.
Associate shipment with the order/package.
Store carrier information.
Store tracking information.
Maintain shipment status.
Track shipment lifecycle.

Example:

CARRIER_SELECTED
        ↓
Shipment Service
        ↓
Create SHIPMENT-7821
        ↓
Carrier: Delhivery
Tracking ID: DL123456
        ↓
Status: CREATED
        ↓
PICKED_UP
        ↓
IN_TRANSIT
        ↓
OUT_FOR_DELIVERY
        ↓
DELIVERED
